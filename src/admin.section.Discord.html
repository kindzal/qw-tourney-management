On this tab you can configure the layout of the weekly schedule message posted to discord.

Select the Round for which you want to post the schedule to Discord by selecting relevant round from the dropdown menu.

You can use Preview post to valdiate the info before posting.

Once happy click Post weekly schedule to Discord button to post the schedule to Discord.

⚠️ Important ⚠️

For QML make sure to go to the Teams tab and populate each team for the week with appropriate players before sending!
