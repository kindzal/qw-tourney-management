This tab is used to record players taking part in the tournament.

The only columns you need edit/enter are Game Nicks and Player.

The Team and Stats are generated automactically.

Game Nicks are a comma-sperated list of nicks players use in their games.

⚠️ Important ⚠️

Game Nicks needs all possible player nicks otherwise player ranking will not work correctly.

Every Game Nick that couldn't be matched will appear on the UnmatchedPlayers tab.

Player is for a display purposes only
