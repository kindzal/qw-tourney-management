This tab is used to record teams taking part in the tournament.

The only columns you need edit/enter are Team Tag, Team Name, Players and Team Tag Display.

Team Tag Display is a bit of a hack due to a slight quirk with Google Sheets.

⚠️ Important ⚠️

If team tag starts with an apostrophe (') you need to add an extra apostrophe to the beginning of the tag in Team Tag Display.

These two columns are used to link team names and team tags throughoutt the sheet and are used for various matching purposes so they need to be set up correctly.

Ask teams participating in the tournament for these upfornt and make sure they use them consistently to avoid dissapointment.

⚠️ Important ⚠️

Below will auto-create teams based on their order on the Players tab. Proceed with caution!

Discord Role ID and Discord Channel Webhook are optional.

They are used for automatic game reminders posting to Discord and for manual availability polls.
